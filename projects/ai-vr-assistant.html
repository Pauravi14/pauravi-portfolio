AI VR Security Assistant

Public portfolio slice of ADA-VR adaptive authentication ideas

Interactive demo of context-based risk scoring for long-lived VR sessions: change inputs (Bluetooth phone trust, network type, login time, minutes since auth) and watch risk level, human-readable explanation, and recommended action update instantly.

  • Threshold model: Low → Critical with mapped security actions
  • Explainable recommendations for recruiters and stakeholders
  • Independent from private university code; deployed on Vercel
